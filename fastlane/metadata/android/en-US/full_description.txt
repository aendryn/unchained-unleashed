Unchained Unleashed is a free and open-source app interfacing with the Real-Debrid and TorBox debrid services. Forget your computer and unchain the power of debrid on mobile! Just log in to your account(s), and you're ready.

It is a community fork of Unchained for Android by LivingWithHippos that adds TorBox support alongside Real-Debrid. All credit for the original app goes to the upstream project and its contributors.

Why use Unchained Unleashed? 📝

- sign in with Real-Debrid and/or TorBox — use either one alone, or both together
- download from all the available hosters (Real-Debrid)
- add torrent files and magnet links, and pick which service handles each one
- a unified torrents list across both services, with per-file link resolution
- foreground notifications that track torrents on both services
- unlock all the features with the private API key
- keep your subscription under control
- free and without ads

Note: this app requires a paid Real-Debrid and/or TorBox account to work
